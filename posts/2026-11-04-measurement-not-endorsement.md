## Which ransomware groups are most active right now?

In our recent-100 leak-site sample (30 Oct – 3 Nov 2026): **N0n** leads
postings, with **Akira**, **Qilin**, **Safepay** and **SilentRansomGroup**
close behind. Group share shifts weekly — the point of a daily series is
that you see the shift, not the news cycle's snapshot.

## Three things this data can't tell you (read before quoting it)

1. **Leak-site posts ≠ total attacks.** They are the subset a group chose to
   publish — a floor. Quiet extortions and unlisted victims never appear.
2. **Group names churn.** Rebrands and splinters make year-over-year group
   rankings unreliable; victim totals are the steadier signal.
3. **Posting date ≠ attack date.** Extortions surface days or weeks after
   intrusion; spikes here describe disclosure, not infection timing.

## What actually reduces ransomware risk (evidence-backed)

The KEV catalog — flaws with *confirmed* exploitation — is dominated by
exactly the internet-facing systems ransomware operators use for entry.
Patch the edge first (VPNs, firewalls, file transfer), keep offline backups,
and test recovery. See our companion explainer on
