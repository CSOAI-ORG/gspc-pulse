Methodology — measurement, not endorsement

  • Models: HuggingFace trending leader + new-with-traction 30-day cohort. Never a downloads leaderboard — download rank is ubiquity, not news.
  • Security: CISA KEV = confirmed exploitation; EPSS = predicted 30-day probability (model-dated daily file). EPSS ≥ 0.1 on a KEV entry is the priority item; young entries score low by model lag; absent score = unscored.
  • Ecosystem: an auth challenge or 402 is a term sheet, not delivery; unreachable is never FAIL; partial rounds are disclosed.
  • Provenance: per-row source URLs live in feed.json; signed artifacts verify under pinned keys — HOW-TO-VERIFY.md.
